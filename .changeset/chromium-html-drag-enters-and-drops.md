**An HTML drag on Chromium enters and drops its target.** A drag whose button was released right after one move to the target, as `dragTo` with its default steps and a hover with the button held make, dropped nothing: the release could go out before Chromium had reported the drag, and the move that started the drag fired no `dragenter`. The move that starts a drag now waits for it and enters the drag at its own point, and the release no longer sends a `dragover` of its own, so a drag of one move fires `dragenter`, `dragover` and `drop` once each, and a move back to where the drag already is fires nothing. A drag whose `dragstart` removes its source no longer waits for a drag Chromium abandoned. Escape pressed during a drag cancels it: the target sees `dragleave` and the source `dragend`, with no `drop` and no key event, and the next drag enters its target again. On Chromium, until a drag starts, each mouse move with a button held reads each document it may start in once more, and the first such move in a document sets up the watch there. A drag from an element in a cross-site frame onto its parent page starts and drops: the moves and the release after a press into a cross-site frame now go to that frame, as a real mouse's do, where before a single move out of the frame went to the parent, and the drag never started.
