**An HTML drag on Chromium enters and drops its target.** A drag whose button was released right after one move to the target, as `dragTo` with its default steps and a hover with the button held make, dropped nothing: the release could go out before Chromium had reported the drag, and the move that started the drag fired no `dragenter`. The move that starts a drag now waits for it and enters the drag at its own point, and the release no longer fires a second `dragover`, so the target sees `dragenter`, `dragover` and `drop` once each. Escape pressed during a drag cancels it: the page sees `dragend` and no `drop`, and no key event. Each mouse move with a button held costs one more round trip on Chromium until a drag starts. A drag from an element in a cross-site frame onto its parent page starts and drops: the moves and the release after a press into a cross-site frame now go to that frame, as a real mouse's do, where before a single move out of the frame went to the parent, and the drag never started.
